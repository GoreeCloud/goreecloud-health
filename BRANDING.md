# GoreeCloud Health — Branding

## Product name

Official product name: **GoreeCloud Health**

Repository: `GoreeCloud/goreecloud-health`

## Visual authority

The canonical source for GoreeCloud logos, application icons, service icons, symbols, illustrations, and related visual identity assets is `GoreeCloud/goreecloud-branding-assets`.

A Health-specific canonical asset was not identified by repository search during the foundation inspection. The foundation therefore uses typography and interface geometry rather than inventing an unofficial production icon. A Health icon/application asset must be established in the canonical branding-assets repository before production visual acceptance.

## Design language

Current shared design target: **GLAZE UI V1.3 — Adaptive Resonance (`1.3.0`)**.

Health may use product-specific health-domain composition and semantic accents, but must preserve GLAZE UI hierarchy, geometry, accessibility, motion, appearance, and material boundaries. Reading/decision surfaces remain solid; glaze effects are reserved for appropriate navigation/control/feedback roles.

## Competitive boundary

Do not copy Samsung Health, Google Fit, Fitbit, or Apple Health logos, icons, screen layouts, trade dress, artwork, or proprietary visual assets. Product inspiration should be expressed through capability quality and original GoreeCloud design.
