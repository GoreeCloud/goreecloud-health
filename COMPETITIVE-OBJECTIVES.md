# GoreeCloud Health — Competitive Objectives

GoreeCloud Health is informed by Samsung Health, Google Fit, Fitbit, and Apple Health as product-category benchmarks. These references define areas to compete on, not assets or interfaces to copy.

## Product objectives

- Match the category expectation for a useful daily health overview while preserving original GoreeCloud information architecture and GLAZE UI presentation.
- Provide broad, understandable health-domain coverage comparable to leading consumer health platforms over time.
- Make data provenance, permissions, export, deletion, and synchronization state easier to understand than typical health dashboards.
- Prefer local-first processing and explicit cloud-purpose authorization where feasible.
- Support Android deeply through Health Connect while maintaining a high-quality web companion.
- Build accessible trend and chart experiences with text/table alternatives and non-color semantics.
- Avoid opaque health scoring when the underlying data, evidence, or calculation cannot support the claim.
- Keep optional coaching/insight features explainable and clearly separated from medical diagnosis.

## Differentiation targets

- **Privacy clarity:** every significant health-data use should have an understandable purpose and control surface.
- **Source truth:** measurements retain source/provenance and uncertainty rather than being silently blended.
- **GoreeCloud integration:** Identity, Privacy Shield, Wardveil, Everkeep, Mesh, Manager, and GLAZE UI remain distinct authorities with evidence-backed integration.
- **Cross-platform continuity:** web and Android share product semantics while using purpose-built platform experiences.
- **Data portability:** export and restore are first-class product requirements rather than afterthoughts.
